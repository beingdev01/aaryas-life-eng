# Structural design feedback

"Make it premium" is not feedback. Each note names the violation, its location, the
measurement and the exact fix: in CRITIQUE.md, Lane C verdicts and CEO fix lists.

The five axes, in order:
1. Hierarchy: one loudest element (the name on a certificate; the guest or event on an
   invitation), at least 1.5x the next size, sharing its weight and colour with nothing.
2. Spacing: equal gaps inside a group, larger gaps between groups, measured in px. The
   dignitary card defect (AAR-120) was 5/13/5 px; the approved fix was 14/14/14 px.
3. Type scale: at most 3 families and about 4 sizes. Smallest text 30 px on a 1080x1920
   master (about 20 px on the 720p copy). Caps tracked 0.12-0.2em; never track body text.
4. Colour discipline: 2-3 colours plus neutrals, only the spec's hex values. Gold for
   rules and display unless the pair passes AA. A hex not in the spec is a defect.
5. Alignment: one axis (centre for ceremonial work), crest centred optically, nothing
   within 10 mm of a print edge or 64 px of a screen edge.

Note format: `[axis] location - what is wrong, measured - exact fix`
- `[spacing] venue block y 1410-1520 - 22 px above, 48 px below - set both to 36 px`
- `[colour] .sig-title - #777777 on #fdfbf4 is 4.32:1 - darken to #6b6b6b (5.15:1)`

Already rejected; never bring back: the AI-redrawn crest (20 Oct 2026); ornaments
painting over text (alumni card); uneven gaps in a text block; blinking, sparkle and
shimmer; Code.SCRIET branding on Aarambh. When a flourish competes with text, remove the
flourish rather than shrink the text.

Taste is not a defect. A choice between two options that both pass these axes goes under
"Not in scope (taste, for the CEO)", each option in one line.
